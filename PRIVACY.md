# Privacy

FocusTube runs entirely on your device.

- **Collected:** nothing is sent to us or any third party. There is no server, account, advertising or analytics SDK.
- **Stored locally (`chrome.storage.local`):** settings, saved topics, queues (video IDs, titles, playback positions), daily viewing totals, a bounded activity log, session summaries, optional search history. You can export it, or delete it all in Settings → Privacy and data.
- **Read from YouTube pages:** page structure (to hide distractions), result titles/channels (for local relevance scoring) and the video element's playback clock (for local viewing time). Nothing from the page leaves the browser.
- **Network:** the extension makes no requests of its own except optional queue thumbnails from `i.ytimg.com`, which you can disable.
- **Never accessed:** credentials, cookies, browsing history outside youtube.com.
